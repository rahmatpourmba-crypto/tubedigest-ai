# Privacy Policy for TubeDigest AI

Last updated: November 18, 2026

## 1. Overview
TubeDigest AI ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a browser extension designed to help users summarize and analyze YouTube video content efficiently. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 2. Information We Collect
TubeDigest AI operates with minimal data collection:
- **Video Content & URL:** When you click the "✨ AI خلاصه هوشمند" button on a YouTube video, the extension reads the public video title and public transcript/description solely to generate an executive summary.
- **License & Account Verification:** If you activate a Pro license, your license key, purchase email, and hardware fingerprint (hash of screen and browser info) are processed solely to prevent unauthorized multi-device sharing and license piracy.
- **No Personal Browsing History:** We do NOT track, collect, or store your browsing history, cookies, personal YouTube account credentials, or search history.

## 3. How We Process Data
- The public video title and text excerpts are processed securely via our cognitive neural engine to produce summaries.
- We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.

## 4. Third-Party Services
- **YouTube:** The extension only operates on public YouTube video pages (`youtube.com/watch?v=...`).
- **Sellix.io:** Secure crypto/payment gateway for optional Pro license purchases.

## 5. User Control & Data Retention
- Your settings (e.g., preferred summary language, free usage counter) are stored locally in your browser via `chrome.storage.local`.
- You can clear all extension data at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing extension storage.

## 6. Contact
For any support or privacy inquiries, contact us at: support@tubedigest.ai
