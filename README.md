# TubeDigest AI 🚀
### Ultra-Premium YouTube AI Video Summarizer & Cognitive Analysis System (Manifest V3)

TubeDigest AI is an ultra-fast, high-contrast browser extension that extracts, synthesizes, and delivers executive summaries of YouTube videos in seconds.

---

## ✨ Features

- 👑 **The Big Takeaway:** Deep executive conclusion uncovering the core argument and grand thesis of the video.
- ⚡ **30-Second Fast Summary:** Rapid executive brief for busy viewers.
- 📌 **Key Highlights & Arguments:** 4+ deep analytical takeaways with concrete facts and turning points.
- 💡 **Actionable Lessons:** Real-world lessons and strategic takeaways for the viewer.
- ⏱️ **Interactive Chapter Timeline:** Clickable timestamp chips that seek directly to key video moments.
- 🌐 **Instant Multi-Language:** One-click instant switching between Persian (فارسی), English, and Arabic (العربية).
- 🔒 **Built-in Monetization & Paywall:** Free trial (3 free video summaries) + seamless crypto checkout via Sellix (USDT).
- 🛡️ **Anti-Piracy HWID Lock:** 1 license per device/account anti-sharing protection.

---

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

- **Manifest V3:** Modern Chrome & Edge extension standard with service worker background isolation.
- **Glassmorphism 4.0 Design:** Pure obsidian dark theme (`#070b14`), neon amber and cyan accents, 18px high-contrast typography.
- **Font:** Embedded Persian Vazirmatn typography with responsive LTR/RTL bidirectional flow.

---

## 📦 Installation for Development

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open Chrome or Edge and go to `chrome://extensions` or `edge://extensions`.
3. Enable **Developer mode** (top right toggle).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select this directory.
5. Open any YouTube video and click **✨ AI خلاصه هوشمند**!
